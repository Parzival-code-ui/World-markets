Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

What we keep when you make an account

  • Your email address, and your name and profile photo if you sign in with Google or give a name.
  • Your picks in My markets, anything you enter about what you bought (dates, prices, quantities and fees), your colour theme and your chart settings, so they follow you to other devices.

That's all. We don't keep a record of which charts you open or what you search for.

Who handles it

Sign-in and storage are run by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase keeps your password, and nobody running World Markets can see it. Your settings are stored so that only your own signed-in account can read or change them.

Market prices and company data come from Yahoo Finance through our own server. Your account details are not sent to Yahoo.

In your browser

The site saves your settings and the last prices it loaded in your browser's local storage, so pages open quickly. Signing out clears your watchlist from that browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we keep it, and for how long

Only to run the features you use: signing you in and showing your own list and holdings on any device. We keep it until you delete your account, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see, correct or erase your data; deleting your account erases it.

Deleting your account

Sign in, open the account menu at the top right and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in and your saved watchlist and settings straight away. If the site asks you to sign in again first, that is a safety check from Firebase.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Don't post personal details there; it's public.

Not investment advice

World Markets is for information and learning only.
